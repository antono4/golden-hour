Why creators love Golden Hour

Questions, answered.

Do I need a token or API key to start?

No. The demo studio runs entirely in your browser with zero authentication. For production-scale API usage you can add a key, but the free creative tools never require one.

What tools are included?

104 AI tools across video (text-to-video, image-to-video, video-to-video, face swap, lip sync, talking photo, animation, UGC ads), image (AI image generator, editor, upscaler, face swap photo), audio (TTS, voice clone, lip sync audio), and effects.

What formats and resolutions are supported?

Exports are MP4 (video) and PNG/JPEG (image). Aspect ratios 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 — optimized for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and ads. Resolution up to 4K depending on the tool.

Is there a watermark?

The free tier adds a small Golden Hour mark. Creator and Teams plans export with no watermark.

Do credits roll over?

Yes — unused credits roll over and are refunded on render errors.
